Community Guidelines

Welcome to the GarbhSarthi community! We want everyone to feel safe, respected, and supported as they share and learn from each other’s journeys. Please read and follow these guidelines to help us maintain a positive environment.

Be Respectful & Kind

  • Respect all members regardless of background, role or experience.
  • Use kind, supportive language even when sharing challenges or disagreements.
  • Seek to listen and encourage rather than judge or criticize.

Protect Privacy

  • Do not share personal information (phone, address, ID, medical data) of yourself or others publicly.
  • Ask permission before sharing another user’s post or story outside the app.

No Harmful or Offensive Content

  • No bullying, harassment, discrimination, hate speech, or threats.
  • Avoid posting graphic or triggering content without proper warnings.
  • No spam, advertising, or unsolicited promotion.

Medical Advice Disclaimer

  • This community is for sharing experiences and support only.
  • Always seek diagnosis and treatment from qualified healthcare providers for medical concerns.

Community Posts

  • All user posts are moderated to maintain quality and safety.
  • We reserve the right to remove posts or ban users who violate these guidelines.
  • Flag inappropriate content using the report option so moderators can review.

Have Fun and Connect!

Share your stories, ask questions, celebrate milestones, and be a positive part of this pregnancy and conception wellness family.
