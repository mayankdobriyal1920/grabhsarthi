Refunds and Cancellations Policy

We value your trust in GarbhSarthi. Please review the following policies regarding subscription refunds and cancellations.

Subscription Cancellation

  • You may cancel your subscription at any time via your account settings or by contacting support@garbhsarthi.com.
  • Cancellations take effect at the end of your current billing cycle. You will retain access until that time.

Refund Policy

  • GarbhSarthi offers a 7-day money-back guarantee for new subscriptions. Please contact us within 7 days of purchase to request a full refund.
  • Refund requests after 7 days are considered on a case-by-case basis and may not be guaranteed.
  • Refunds are issued to the original payment method and may take up to 10 business days to process depending on your bank or payment provider.

Free Trials

  • Free trials are offered for eligible promotional periods. If you do not cancel before the trial period ends, your subscription will automatically begin and billing will occur.
  • You can cancel any time during the trial to avoid charges.

Changes to Policy

  • We may update these policies from time to time. We will notify you via email or app notice when substantial changes occur.
  • Continued use after changes means you accept the updated policy.

Contact Us

  • If you have questions or want to request a refund or cancellation, please email support@garbhsarthi.com.
