Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to GarbhSarthi. This policy describes what information we collect, why, and how we protect it. By using our app or site, you agree to this policy.

What Information We Collect

  • Account details: Name, email, phone number (if you sign up).
  • Profile information: Age, pregnancy stage, trying to conceive status, language preferences, and other voluntarily entered wellness or health information.
  • Usage data: App usage statistics, device type, IP address, page views, crash/error logs, browser/device info. This helps us improve our service.
  • Wellness data you log: Data you provide in trackers (mood, cycle, symptoms, etc.) is stored securely and kept confidential.
  • Payment: We do not store credit card or UPI info; all payments processed securely through payment gateways like Razorpay.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide personalized health tracking, routines, reminders, and wellness content tailored for pregnancy/conception.
  • To operate and improve GarbhSarthi app and site features.
  • To respond to support queries, deliver classes, and community engagement.
  • To comply with legal, regulatory, or payment requirements.
  • To send important notifications, service updates, and wellness tips.
  • We do not use your information for unsolicited marketing, nor do we sell user data to third-parties.

How Your Data is Protected

  • All personal and health data is transmitted via secure, encrypted connections (SSL/HTTPS).
  • Our staff and partners are bound by confidentiality obligations.
  • We never publish your data or posts outside the community (unless you post voluntarily in a shared group).

Your Choices & Rights

  • You may access, edit, or delete your profile and logged data any time from account settings.
  • You can request complete deletion of your account and associated data by emailing support@garbhsarthi.com.
  • You may opt out of notifications or revoke permissions from the app or your device.
  • To protect children, we do not knowingly collect data from users under 16 without guardian consent.

Data Retention Policy

  • We retain personal data for as long as you have an active account or as needed for compliance and support.
  • When deleted, your data is removed from our systems in a reasonable timeframe unless required for legal or accounting records.

Third-Party Links & Integrations

  • We may provide links to other wellness, medical, or partner websites. We are not responsible for third-party privacy or security practices.

Changes to Our Privacy Policy

  • This privacy policy may be updated. Changes are posted here, and continued use of GarbhSarthi means acceptance of updates.

Contact Us

  • For privacy-related questions, contact support@garbhsarthi.com.
